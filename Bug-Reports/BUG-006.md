# BUG-006 — Response Actions Missing in Reopened History Conversations

## Bug ID
BUG-006

## Title
Copy and Retry buttons are missing from AI responses in reopened History conversations

## Description
When an existing conversation is reopened from the **History** section in the EchoGPT Chrome Extension, the response-level action buttons available for AI responses are not displayed.

In the Chrome Extension, AI responses normally provide **Copy** and **Retry** actions. However, these actions are missing when viewing a previously saved conversation from History.

The issue was reproduced on the **Chrome Extension**. The same previously saved conversation was checked on the **Android App**, where the **Copy** and **Retry** actions were available for AI responses.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the **EchoGPT Chrome Extension**.
2. Open or start a conversation containing an AI response.
3. Verify that the AI response displays the **Copy** and **Retry** buttons.
4. Open the **History** section.
5. Select the same previously saved conversation.
6. Locate an AI response in the reopened conversation.
7. Observe the area below the AI response.

## Expected Result

Previously saved AI responses should retain the available response actions, including **Copy** and **Retry**, when the conversation is reopened from History.

## Actual Result

The **Copy** and **Retry** buttons are available for AI responses in the current conversation but are missing when the same conversation is reopened from History in the Chrome Extension.

The same conversation was checked on the Android App, where the **Copy** and **Retry** buttons were available.
