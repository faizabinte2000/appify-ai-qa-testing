# BUG-001 — AI Fails to Maintain Conversation Context

## Bug ID
BUG-001

## Title
AI fails to maintain conversation context when responding to a follow-up question

## Description
The AI does not appear to retain or use the context from its immediately preceding response when the user asks a follow-up question referring to that response.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the **Ask Anything** feature.
2. Send: `What is software testing?`
3. Wait for the response to fully complete.
4. Wait for some additional time.
5. Send: `What are the main types you mentioned?`

## Expected Result
The AI should understand that “the main types you mentioned” refers to the types of software testing listed in its immediately preceding response and provide a relevant answer.

## Actual Result
The AI stated that it was not aware of any previous conversation discussing “main types” and asked the user to provide additional context.

## Severity
Major

## Priority
High

## Environment
- Device: realme 8 Pro
- OS: Android 12
- App Version: 1.0.8
- AI Model: EchoChat
- Network: Wi-Fi
- Date Tested: 23 September 2026
