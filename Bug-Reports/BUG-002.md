# BUG-002 — Incorrect History Timestamp

## Bug ID
BUG-002

## Title
History displays an incorrect conversation timestamp on Android App

## Description
The **History** section of the EchoGPT Android App displays a timestamp that does not match the actual time when the conversation/message was sent. The displayed time was approximately 6 hours earlier than the actual message time.

This issue was reproduced on the **Android App** but was **not observed on the Chrome Extension** during testing.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the **EchoGPT Android App**.
2. Open the **Ask Anything** feature.
3. Send a message.
4. Note the time when the message is sent.
5. Open the **History** section.
6. Locate the conversation corresponding to the sent message.
7. Compare the timestamp displayed in History with the actual message time.

## Expected Result

The History section should display the correct date and local time corresponding to when the conversation was created or updated.

## Actual Result

The message was sent at approximately **2:28 PM**, but the corresponding conversation in History displayed **08:27 AM**, approximately 6 hours earlier.

The issue was observed on the **Android App** and was **not reproduced on the Chrome Extension**.

## Severity
Minor

## Priority
Medium

## Environment

- Platform: Android App
- Device: realme 8 Pro
- OS: Android 12
- App Version: 1.0.8
- AI Model: EchoChat
- Network: Wi-Fi
- Date Tested: 24 September 2026

## Platform Comparison

| Platform | Result |
|---|---|
