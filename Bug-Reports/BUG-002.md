# BUG-002 — Incorrect History Timestamp

## Bug ID
BUG-002

## Title
History displays an incorrect conversation timestamp

## Description
The History section displays a timestamp that does not match the actual time when the conversation/message was sent. The displayed time is approximately 6 hours earlier than the actual message time.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the **Ask Anything** feature.
2. Send a message.
3. Note the time when the message is sent.
4. Open the **History** section.
5. Locate the conversation corresponding to the sent message.
6. Compare the timestamp displayed in History with the actual message time.

## Expected Result
The History section should display the correct date and local time corresponding to when the conversation was created or updated.

## Actual Result
The message was sent at approximately **2:28 PM**, but the corresponding conversation in History displayed **08:27 AM**, approximately 6 hours earlier.

## Severity
Minor

## Priority
Medium

## Environment
- Device: realme 8 Pro
- OS: Android 12
- App Version: 1.0.8
- AI Model: EchoChat
- Network: Wi-Fi
- Date Tested: 23 September 2026
